# Data Processing Agreement (Template)

> **Draft template — pending legal review.** This is a starting point for an agreement between GGHighTech LLC and a school or district. It must be reviewed by an attorney and completed before signature. Many districts have their own required data privacy agreement (for example a state or consortium standard form); where a district's form is used, it governs.

**Version:** October 1, 2026

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is between **[SCHOOL OR DISTRICT NAME]** (the "School") and **GGHighTech LLC**, a [STATE] limited liability company (the "Provider"). It applies to the Provider's School Drop-off & Pick-up service (the "Service").

## 1. Definitions

- **Student Data:** any information about a student that the Provider receives or creates while providing the Service, including information that is part of an education record under FERPA.
- **Personal Information:** Student Data and information about parents, guardians and school staff.
- **Subprocessor:** a third party the Provider uses to process Personal Information. See [Vendors & Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors).

## 2. Roles and ownership

2.1 The School owns and controls all Personal Information. The Provider processes it only on the School's behalf and documented instructions. The School's instructions are this Agreement, the School's configuration of the Service, and actions taken by its authorized administrators.

2.2 For FERPA purposes, the Provider is designated a **school official with a legitimate educational interest** under 34 CFR § 99.31(a)(1). The Provider is under the School's direct control with respect to the use and maintenance of education records.

## 3. Permitted use

3.1 The Provider uses Personal Information only to provide, secure and support the Service for the School.

3.2 The Provider will **not**:

- sell Personal Information;
- use it for targeted advertising;
- build profiles of students for non-school purposes; or
- disclose it, except to Subprocessors under section 6, as the School directs, or as required by law.

3.3 The Provider complies with FERPA and, where applicable, COPPA, section 1006.1494 of the Florida Statutes, and the student privacy laws of the School's state. [ATTORNEY: list applicable state laws.]

## 4. Data collected

The Service collects only the categories described in the Provider's [Student Data Privacy Policy](/legal/student-data-privacy). Device location is checked at the moment of a drop-off or pick-up request and is not stored.

## 5. Security

5.1 The Provider maintains reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, as described in the [Security Policy](/legal/security). These include:

- encryption in transit;
- role-based access;
- school data isolation;
- two-step verification for administrators;
- one-time pickup verification codes; and
- an append-only audit log available to the School.

5.2 Provider personnel with access to Personal Information are bound by confidentiality obligations and receive access only as needed. [CONFIRM background checks / training if required by the School.]

## 6. Subprocessors

6.1 The School authorizes the Subprocessors listed on the [Vendors & Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page.

6.2 The Provider will give the School **[X] days'** notice before adding or replacing a Subprocessor that receives Personal Information. During that period the School may object on reasonable grounds.

6.3 Each Subprocessor is bound in writing by obligations at least as protective as this Agreement. The Provider remains responsible for its Subprocessors.

## 7. Incidents

7.1 The Provider will notify the School of any unauthorized access to or acquisition of Personal Information without unreasonable delay, and **no later than [X — e.g. 72 hours; not more than 10 days, consistent with s. 501.171(6), F.S.]** after determining that it occurred.

7.2 The notice will describe:

- what happened;
- the information and individuals involved;
- the containment and remediation steps taken; and
- a contact person.

7.3 The Provider will cooperate with the School's investigation and notifications, and will bear the reasonable costs of notifications caused by its breach of this Agreement. [ATTORNEY]

See [Incident Response](/legal/incident-response).

## 8. Access, correction and deletion requests

The Service lets the School's administrators export any student's complete record and correct or delete records. The Provider will help the School respond to requests from parents, eligible students and regulators within [X] days of the School's request.

## 9. Retention, return and deletion

9.1 The School controls retention through the Service, as described in the [Data Retention & Deletion Policy](/legal/data-retention).

9.2 Within **[X — e.g. 60] days** after this Agreement ends, or earlier on the School's written request, the Provider will make a full export available to the School, then permanently delete the School's Personal Information. It will confirm the deletion in writing. Backups expire within [X] days.

## 10. Audits

On reasonable notice, and no more than once a year unless an incident has occurred, the Provider will answer the School's security questionnaire and provide relevant documentation. School administrators may review the School's audit log in the Service at any time.

## 11. Term and precedence

This Agreement lasts as long as the Provider processes the School's Personal Information. If it conflicts with the Provider's Terms of Service or Privacy Policy, this Agreement prevails.
