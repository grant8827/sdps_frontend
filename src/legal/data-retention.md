# Data Retention & Deletion Policy

> **Draft — pending legal review.** Items in [BRACKETS] must be completed before this document is relied on.

**Last updated:** October 1, 2026

This policy explains how long **GGHighTech LLC** keeps information in School Drop-off & Pick-up (the "Service"), and how schools export and delete it. Schools decide how long their records are kept, within the limits below, so they can follow their own and their district's records policies.

## Who decides

The school (or district) owns its records and sets retention. We keep information only as long as the school's settings and agreement allow, or as the law requires.

## Retention periods

| Information | Kept | Controlled by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current students' records (profile, enrollment, attendance, guardians) | While the student is active at the school | The school |
| Removed students | Until the school restores or permanently deletes them, or the retention period the school sets runs out (minimum 30 days) | **Data & Privacy → Retention** |
| Finished drop-off/pick-up requests | Until the retention period the school sets runs out (minimum 30 days); kept otherwise | **Data & Privacy → Retention** |
| Pending drop-off/pick-up requests | Until decided; never removed by retention | — |
| Pickup verification codes | Only while the pickup is pending; erased when it's decided | Automatic |

| Sign-in sessions | Up to 24 hours | Automatic |

Retention settings are applied automatically every day. An administrator can also apply them immediately and see in advance how many records would be deleted.

## Exporting

School administrators can, at any time:

- **export one student's complete record** (profile and photo, enrollment history, guardians and pickup rights, guardian requests, attendance, drop-off/pick-up history and that student's audit trail) to answer a parent's request or a records request; and
- **export all of the school's data** (students, parents, staff, classes, attendance, drop-off/pick-up history).

Exports never include passwords, two-step verification secrets or pickup codes. Every export is recorded in the audit log.

## Deleting

- **Removing** a student takes them off class lists and the queue but keeps the record, so it can be restored.
- **Permanent deletion** erases a removed student's record, photo, enrollments, guardian links and requests, attendance and drop-off/pick-up history. It cannot be undone, and the administrator must type the student's full name to confirm.
- After a permanent deletion, the audit log keeps only the student's initials and student number for that event, so the school can show the deletion happened. ## Requests from parents

Parents and guardians make review, correction and deletion requests to their school, which uses the tools above. If we receive a request directly, we pass it to the school and help it respond.
