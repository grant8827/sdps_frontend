School Drop-off & Pick-up (the "Service") is a website and mobile app operated by **GGHighTech LLC** ("we", "us"). Schools use it to run drop-off, pick-up, attendance and school-to-family messaging. This policy explains what information the Service handles, why, and the choices available to you.

Information about **students** is covered in more detail by our [Student Data Privacy Policy](/legal/student-data-privacy), which takes precedence over this policy wherever the two differ.

## Who uses the Service

- **Schools and districts** — our customers. They decide who has an account and which students are in the Service.
- **School staff** — administrators, front desk staff, teachers and district administrators.
- **Parents and guardians** — adults a school (or another authorized guardian, with school approval) has connected to a student.

**Students do not have accounts and do not use the Service.** The Service is not directed to children.

## Information we handle

| Category | Examples | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account information | Name, email, phone number, role, school, password (stored only as a salted hash) | The school, or the person when they are invited |
| Student information | Name, date of birth, student number, photo, grade, class, teacher, attendance, drop-off/pick-up status and history | The school |
| Guardian relationships | Which adults are linked to which students, their relationship, whether they may pick up | The school; parents may request additions, which the school approves |
| Messages | Notices between the school, teachers and parents | The people who send them |
| Location at drop-off/pick-up | Your device's location at the moment you tap Drop Off or Pick Up | Your device, with your permission |
| Security records | Sign-in history, network (IP) address, two-step verification status, audit log of sensitive actions | Generated by the Service |

### Location

When you request a drop-off or pick-up, the app reads your device's location once and the server checks whether you are inside the school's pickup area. **We do not track your location continuously and we do not store your coordinates** — only whether the request was accepted. You can withdraw location permission in your device settings; drop-off and pick-up requests will then not be available.

## How we use information

We use information only to provide the Service to the school: to show the right students to the right adults, process drop-off and pick-up requests, record attendance, deliver messages, keep accounts secure, keep an audit trail, provide support, and meet legal obligations.

**We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not build marketing profiles.** We do not use student information for any purpose other than providing the Service to the school.

## Who we share information with

- **Within the school.** People in a school see only what their role allows. For example, a parent sees only their own children, and a teacher sees only their own class.
- **Service providers** that host and run the Service on our behalf, under contract and only as needed. These are listed on our [Vendors & Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page.
- **When required by law**, or to protect someone's safety, and then only to the extent required.
- **If the company is sold or merged**, only under terms at least as protective as this policy, and with notice to schools.

## Security

We protect information with encryption in transit, strict role-based access, two-step verification for administrators, location checks on drop-off and pick-up requests, sign-in lockouts and a tamper-resistant audit log. See our [Security Policy](/legal/security).

## Retention and deletion

Schools control how long their records are kept, and can export or permanently delete a student's records at any time. See our [Data Retention & Deletion Policy](/legal/data-retention).

## Your choices and rights

- **Parents and guardians** may ask their school to review, correct or delete information about themselves or their child. Because the school controls these records, we will direct requests we receive to the school and help the school respond.
- **Account holders** can turn on two-step verification, change their password and manage location permission at any time.

## Children

Students do not create accounts or use the Service. Information about students is provided by their school for school purposes, as described in the [Student Data Privacy Policy](/legal/student-data-privacy).
