This policy describes how **GGHighTech LLC** handles information about K–12 students in School Drop-off & Pick-up (the "Service"). It applies in addition to our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) and, for students, takes precedence over it.

## Our commitments

1. **The school owns and controls student data.** We act on the school's behalf and under its direction.
2. **We use student data only to provide the Service to the school.** Specifically, we use it for drop-off and pick-up, attendance, the class roster, and school-to-family communication.
3. **We never sell student data**, never use it for targeted advertising, and never build a profile of a student for any purpose other than school purposes.
4. **We collect the minimum needed.** We do not collect grades, health records, disciplinary records, Social Security numbers, or biometric data.
5. **Students do not use the Service** and have no accounts. Only school staff and authorized adults do.
6. **We delete or return student data** when the school asks, and when our contract with the school ends.

## Student information in the Service

- Name, date of birth (optional), student number (optional) and photo (optional).
- Grade, class, classroom and teacher, for each school year.
- Daily attendance status (present, absent, sick, suspended, holiday, late) and who recorded it.
- Drop-off and pick-up history: who requested it, when, and who released the child. It also records how the adult was verified, either a one-time pickup code or an administrator's ID check with a written reason.
- The adults authorized for the child, their relationship, and whether each may pick up.

## FERPA

When we receive education records from a school, we do so as a **"school official" with a legitimate educational interest** under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), 34 CFR § 99.31(a)(1). This means:

- the school has direct control over our use and maintenance of the records;
- we use the records only for the purposes the school authorized; and
- we do not re-disclose them except as the school directs or the law permits.

Parents and eligible students exercise FERPA rights (to inspect, request correction, and so on) through the school. The Service lets the school export a complete record for a student so it can answer such requests.

## Florida law

For Florida schools, we intend to meet the requirements that apply to operators of K–12 online services under section **1006.1494, Florida Statutes** (Student Online Personal Information Protection). [ATTORNEY: confirm applicability and complete this list.] In particular:

- no targeted advertising, profiling for non-school purposes, or sale of student information;
- reasonable security procedures appropriate to the information; and
- deletion of a student's information within a reasonable time when the school requests it.

For security incidents, we follow the notification requirements of the Florida Information Protection Act, **section 501.171, Florida Statutes**, as described in our [Incident Response](/legal/incident-response) page.

## Parent and guardian controls

- A parent may ask the school to authorize another adult. That adult gets **no access** until a school administrator approves the request.
- Every pickup needs a **one-time code** that only the requesting adult's phone shows. The teacher must enter it before releasing the child.
- Parents can ask the school to review, correct or delete their child's information.

## Retention and deletion

Schools can export a student's full record, permanently delete a removed student, and set automatic retention periods. When our agreement with a school ends, we return or delete its data as described in the [Data Retention & Deletion Policy](/legal/data-retention) and the [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/data-processing-agreement).
