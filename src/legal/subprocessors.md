# Vendors & Subprocessors

**Last updated:** October 1, 2026

These are the outside companies involved in running SDPMPlus, and what each one receives. **GGHighTech LLC** reviews every vendor before it receives any personal information. Each vendor must protect the information under written terms at least as protective as our own commitments to schools.

## Current vendors

| Vendor | Purpose | Information it receives | Student information? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Railway** | Hosts the application servers and the database | All Service data, stored and processed on our behalf | Yes — hosted, not used by the vendor |
| **OpenStreetMap Nominatim** (OpenStreetMap Foundation) | Turns a school's address into map coordinates for the pickup area | The school's street address, only when an administrator saves it | No |
| **Apple App Store / Google Play** | Distributes the mobile app | Standard app-store download information; no Service data | No |

## What we do *not* use

The Service currently does **not** use:

- third-party analytics or tracking;
- advertising networks;
- crash-reporting services;
- push-notification services;
- email or SMS providers;
- third-party map or location tracking SDKs.

Location checks happen between your device and our server only. The QR code used to set up two-step verification is generated inside your browser; no outside service is involved.

## Before adding a vendor

Before any new vendor receives personal information, for example the planned **live chat** provider, an email/SMS provider, or crash reporting, we:

1. confirm that the vendor is necessary and that it will receive only the minimum information needed;
2. review its security and privacy practices, and where it stores data;
3. sign written terms. These require the vendor to use the data only for our purpose, to never sell it or use it for advertising, to protect it with reasonable security, to notify us of incidents promptly, and to delete it when no longer needed. They also apply to student data the protections required by FERPA and by state law, including section 1006.1494, Florida Statutes;
4. update this page, and notify schools before the vendor starts receiving their data, as required by our agreements.
