## What we do *not* use

The Service currently does **not** use:

- third-party analytics or tracking;
- advertising networks;
- crash-reporting services;
- push-notification services;
- email or SMS providers;
- third-party map or location tracking SDKs.

Location checks happen between your device and our server only. The QR code used to set up two-step verification is generated inside your browser; no outside service is involved.

## Before adding a vendor

Before any new vendor receives personal information, for example the planned **live chat** provider, an email/SMS provider, or crash reporting, we:

1. confirm that the vendor is necessary and that it will receive only the minimum information needed;
2. review its security and privacy practices, and where it stores data;
3. sign written terms. These require the vendor to use the data only for our purpose, to never sell it or use it for advertising, to protect it with reasonable security, to notify us of incidents promptly, and to delete it when no longer needed. They also apply to student data the protections required by FERPA and by state law, including section 1006.1494, Florida Statutes [ATTORNEY: confirm];
4. update this page, and notify schools [X] days before the vendor starts receiving their data, as required by our agreements.
